This is what the care team sees. One list per clinic each morning: who is due, overdue or unreachable, sorted only by days overdue and contact attempts, never by a clinical risk score. Each row has one-tap actions: call, reschedule, request a village health nurse (VHN) visit, notify the doctor. Families get WhatsApp reminders in Tamil or English with Confirm and Reschedule buttons, or a voice call if they don't use WhatsApp. If they reply with a voice note, Sarvam's Saaras v3 transcribes it and the reply is logged as an action (confirm, reschedule, moved, wrong number). Reminders are bundled: one message per family for the clinic's next session day, with the mother's and the baby's next visits in one trip and the doses named, as in the J-PAL Haryana trial where reminders about the next vaccine were part of a package that raised measles vaccination about 55 percent. Here, Meena's voice-note reschedule moves both visits to Saturday on the nurse's list without anyone typing it in. This flow runs live in the prototype.
