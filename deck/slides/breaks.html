Pregnancy to age two is more than a dozen scheduled contacts across two departments. At delivery the mother's record stays with obstetrics and the baby's begins in paediatrics, so no single team sees the whole journey. In hospitals and clinics, especially private ones, follow-up still runs on registers, spreadsheets and phone calls; a miss is noticed only when the family returns late, or never. Much of it is preventable: in an Indian study the top reasons for missed vaccines were no reminder (32.9%) and forgetfulness (26.6%). Even children who are vaccinated are often late: 34.8% of measles doses were delayed.
