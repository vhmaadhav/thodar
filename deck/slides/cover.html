Thodar is a follow-up desk for hospital and clinic care teams. It ties mother and baby into one care thread from the first antenatal (ANC) visit to the child's second birthday, so no visit, postnatal check or vaccine dose is silently missed. It is assistive and non-clinical: doctors set the plan, Thodar makes sure it happens. Track: Maternal and Women's Health. Primary user: doctor and care team. Use case: Patient Follow-up and Continuity of Care. Team Highest in the Room is led clinically by a practising paediatrician, Dr Sughapriya V. C, who owns the mother-to-baby handover this product is built around. The thread across the top of every slide is the product's idea; the knot moves forward one step per slide.
