Thodar · one follow-up thread for every mother and baby

08 / 08

Mapping to the use case brief. Early identification: a daily list of due and overdue visits so teams act before a miss becomes loss to care. Visibility across fragmented information: one thread per mother–baby pair from registers, Excel, HIS exports and digitised paper. Coordinated follow-up: the obstetrics-to-paediatrics handover has an owner, with VHN visits and doctor notifications in one tap. Simplified workflows: no new data entry; families respond on WhatsApp or a phone call. Scope: assistive only. Pilot over 90 days at our paediatrician's practice, measured against a baseline from the same clinic's registers. The headline measure is families brought back into care: overdue visits that happened after Thodar followed up, which the prototype already counts. Also: on-time ANC attendance, PNC completion at 48 hours and day 7, on-time vaccine doses from 6 weeks to 9 months, median days overdue, and staff hours per week spent on follow-up calls. How Thodar differs from existing programmes: PICME, JANANI and U-WIN serve field nurses and ASHAs with separate maternal and child modules and need manual entry; Kilkari and mMitra send one-way voice messages to mothers. Thodar serves hospital and clinic teams, including private ones, links mother and baby automatically at birth, needs no new data entry and lets families confirm or reschedule. Team: Dr Sughapriya V.
