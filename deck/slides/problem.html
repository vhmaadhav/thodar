Starting care is solved; staying in care is not. Nationally 95.9% of women start antenatal care but only 65.2% complete four visits, so about one in three who start drop off. Over 96% of children receive a first vaccine but only 87.1% are fully immunised by card and 71.8% receive the second measles dose (NFHS-6, 2023-24). Tamil Nadu, among India's best performers, moved backwards between NFHS-5 and NFHS-6: four-visit ANC 90.6% to 87.6%, full vaccination 90.4% to 89.7%, and urban mothers (86.2%) now do worse than rural (88.6%). Urban care runs through hospitals and private clinics, which is exactly where Thodar works.
