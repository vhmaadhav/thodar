Scope · assistive, not clinical

Doctors set the plan. Thodar makes sure it happens.

No diagnosis

No risk scores

No reading of results

No medical advice

AI only reads paper and talks to families. Due dates and order come from fixed rules. Every reply passes two locks, rules and a model: if either sees a health concern, a nurse calls.

Thodar stays outside every out-of-scope item in the brief: it does not diagnose, recommend treatment, provide clinical decision support, score clinical risk, interpret medical data or give autonomous advice. The worklist is ordered only by days overdue and contact attempts. If a doctor tags a pregnancy as needing closer follow-up, Thodar simply applies the shorter interval the doctor set. The voice agent is limited to scheduling actions through tool calls; any health question ends with a handoff to a nurse, and every transcript is logged for audit.
