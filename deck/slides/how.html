How Thodar works

One thread per mother–baby pair.

1

Read

Registers, Excel sheets, HIS exports and photos of paper registers. No new data entry.

2

Link

Matches records on PICME / RCH ID, ABHA and phone into one thread.

3

Schedule

At delivery, the baby's schedule is created beside the mother's PNC plan.

4

Worklist

Each morning: due, overdue, unreachable. Sorted by days overdue.

5

Reach

WhatsApp and Tamil or English voice calls. Replies update the list.

04 / 08

Five steps. Read: Thodar ingests what teams already keep (registers, Excel, HIS exports, photos of paper registers digitised with Sarvam Vision and verified by a nurse). Link: probabilistic record linking on PICME/RCH ID, ABHA and phone builds one thread per mother–baby pair. Schedule: doctor-set plans and the national ANC, PNC and immunisation schedules become due dates; at delivery the baby's schedule is created automatically next to the mother's postnatal plan. Worklist: every morning nurses see who is due, overdue or unreachable, sorted by days overdue, with one-tap actions: call, reschedule, request a village health nurse visit, notify the doctor. Reach: WhatsApp quick-replies and Tamil/English voice calls; replies flow back into the worklist. Thodar complements government systems (PICME in Tamil Nadu, JANANI launched May 2026, U-WIN) rather than duplicating them.
