# Thodar — demo

**Thodar** (Tamil: "continue, follow through") is a follow-up desk for hospital and clinic care teams. It ties
mother and baby into one care thread, from the first antenatal visit to the child's second birthday, so no
visit, postnatal check or vaccine dose is silently missed.

Health-a-thon 2026 · Maternal & Women's Health · Patient Follow-up & Continuity of Care ·
Team *Highest in the Room*.

## Try it

1. **Worklist**: press *Send today's WhatsApp reminders*. One Tamil message per family appears on the demo
   phone: the clinic's next session day, mother and baby in one trip.
2. Reply as the family: tap a button, a typed reply such as *"இன்னைக்கு வர முடியாது, சனிக்கிழமை வரேன்"*
   (can't come today, will come Saturday), or a 🎤 voice note.
3. Try *"குழந்தைக்கு காய்ச்சல்"* (baby has fever): it goes to a nurse and the family is told a person will call.
4. Open a family for the mother–baby thread, consent and data rights; *Insights* for the drop-off funnel.

**All data is synthetic and resets on every restart.** Thodar is assistive, not clinical: it schedules and
reminds; it never diagnoses, scores risk or gives advice. Without a Sarvam key the demo runs on rules only
(voice notes and photo reading are then off).
